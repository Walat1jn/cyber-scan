Why this matters

Advertisers, data brokers, and attackers all use traits like these. Attackers use them to craft convincing scams, like a fake update message that names your exact phone and browser.

What you can do

  • Pick a browser with tracking protection. Safari, Firefox, and Brave all limit fingerprinting. Notice the blue "hidden" rows above.
  • A VPN is not enough. It hides your network address, but not your screen, browser, or graphics chip.
  • Review site permissions for location, camera, and notifications in your browser settings.
  • Keep your software updated. Old versions make you easier to pick out and easier to attack.
  • Think before you scan. You just scanned a QR code to get here. Unknown QR codes can lead to fake login pages, so check the web address before you type anything.
